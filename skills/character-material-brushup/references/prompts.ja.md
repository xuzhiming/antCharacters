# 仕上げ指示の組み立て

画像の役割、完成品質、今回の材質、保持する形を指定します。以下は固定全文ではありません。今回の観察と不足に合わせて必要な段落を選びます。第三者の参考画像やURLは渡さず、観察した表現を文章にします。

## 共通の仕上げ

> Finish this asset to the standard of a fully painted VTuber / Live2D character illustration viewed up close. Preserve crisp, delicate structural linework while developing the color inside each existing form: broad layered color transitions, clear overlap accents, soft local modeling, and carefully placed material-specific highlights. Keep quiet luminous areas between focal details. Give the face, hair, exposed skin and clothing a consistent level of finish, with distinct surface qualities for each material. Preserve the character's identity, base palette, patterns and construction. The source defines the design, not a ceiling on painting quality. Make the asset read as a finished standalone illustration without relying on background effects or dramatic scene lighting.

## 顔と露出肌

新規頭部素材では、正面の傾き補正とニュートラル表情の指定を併用します。塗りの指示で元絵の表情を復元しません。既存素材の塗りだけの修正では、その素材で採用済みの表情を指定します。

> Keep the facial proportions, characteristic eye contours, complexion and iris identity. Use the expression specified for this output. Paint connected layers of color across the upper eyelids, outer eye corners, lower eyelid rims, cheeks, ears, nose and mouth area. Vary the width and intensity of this warmth, blending into the original complexion while retaining clear luminous skin areas. Render the existing eyelash shapes with deep chromatic tones and fine colored sheen; keep their shape and count. Develop depth within the existing iris design through darker upper color, luminous lower color and organized reflections. Finish the lips in the specified expression with delicate color, soft modeling and small controlled gloss. Carry the same skin treatment onto the neck, hands and other exposed skin where relevant. Do not substitute uniform pink tint, dark facial shadows or extra sparkle decorations for this local finishing.

肌の血色を機械的に最大化しません。観察した色の関係を元の肌色へ適応し、特定のピンクや瞳色を固定しません。目・口の形の保持と、その内部・周囲の色を仕上げることは両立させます。

## 髪

> Preserve the hairstyle, full length, silhouette, parting, existing locks and accessories. Paint layered color within those locks: smooth hue and value transitions across the broad surfaces, chromatic depth at the roots and overlaps, broad soft sheen with a few sharper illustrated reflections following curvature. Let recessed hair remain quieter but readable. Keep the original hair color recognizable. Build finish through the painting of existing forms, without adding strands, subdividing locks or covering every lock with an identical white highlight stripe.

## 衣装

> Preserve the garment silhouette, construction, existing folds, seams, patterns and accessories. Finish the surfaces with layered color transitions, form-following fold shading and material-specific reflections. [Describe only materials present: broad soft modeling on matte fabric; flowing reflections on smooth fabric; structured restrained sheen on leather; narrow crisp highlights on metal.] Keep pale fabrics chromatic and dark fabrics readable. Render edges, cuffs and overlaps with depth. Preserve quiet areas of cloth and the original motifs. Do not add new folds, seams, embroidery or hardware to create detail, and do not give every surface the same glossy finish.
